# Bug hunt, 2026-10-16

Method: reproduce first (a failing test or a scripted run), then fix, then keep the test. Sources: the 13 end-to-end tests that failed on the original code, a read-through of `main.ts` and my milestone-1 code, and a scripted sweep (levels 1 to 20, all 5 songs, Learn, the piano, bad uploads, damaged saved data, accessibility names; desktop and phone, English and Persian).

Result: 174 end-to-end tests pass on all three projects (before: 21 of 120 failed on the original code), 345 unit tests pass, `tsc` and `npm run build` are clean.

## The 13 failing upstream tests

None was an app bug. Two causes:

| Tests | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 459, 588 (all three projects) and three more that passed for the wrong reason | They read `Beat N: playing …` lines from the console. The app stopped printing them long ago, so there were 0 events. Three of the tests passed vacuously: zero logs meant zero errors. | The app now fires a `trainer:beat` event; the tests record it, wait for enough beats instead of sleeping, and assert there is at least one beat. |
| 83, 109, 126, 319, 381 (phones) | The tests close the options panel by clicking the score. On phones the panel covers the middle of the score, so the click hit the panel. | Tests close it with `#optionsClose`. |
| 280 (phones) | `label[for=bpm]` was `display: none` on phones, which also removes the tempo field's visible name for screen readers. This one is a real accessibility bug. | The label is visually hidden (still present) on phones. |

## Checked and fine

- Space on the focused Start button toggles exactly once.
- Notes played in the quiz do not reach the exercise: matching only runs while an exercise plays, and Learn stops it.
- Levels 1 to 20, the 5 songs, the 5 lessons, the quiz and the piano run without errors on desktop and phone, in English and Persian. No horizontal scrolling.
- Damaged saved practice state, note statistics, language and device values are handled.

## Known and left alone

- Piano sounds still load from `tonejs.github.io` (needs internet; now handled gracefully).
- Dev-tool `npm audit` findings (Vite, Vitest, gh-pages).
- `src/playback/` and `src/curriculum/` are upstream modules that `main.ts` does not use; they have their own copies of the same logic (and of bugs 2 and 7). They are untouched.
