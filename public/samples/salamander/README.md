# Piano sounds

The 30 sample files here are the "Salamander Grand Piano" (Yamaha C5), recorded by Alexander Holm and licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Source of the sound:
https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3. The files are the MP3 versions that Tone.js publishes at
https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/; they were copied here once so that the app needs no internet.
